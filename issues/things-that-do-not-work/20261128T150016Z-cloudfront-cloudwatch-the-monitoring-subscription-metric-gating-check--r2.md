Cause 1 of 1: the subscription GATING boundary for CloudFront's additional metrics is unobservable. A distribution that reports Status=Deployed does not serve viewer requests at the address the emulator announces as its DomainName, and no AWS/CloudFront metric series is ever published for either a subscribed distribution or an unsubscribed one, so the check that drives identical traffic through a subscribed distribution and an unsubscribed control and then compares which series exist never evaluates a single comparison. Failing check: cloudfront__cloudwatch.obs-cloudfront-1-additional-metrics-withheld-without-monitoring-subscription.

AWS:      Smithy (services/service-com-amazonaws-cloudfront-cloudfront2020-05-31) models only the control plane of the opt-in: CreateMonitoringSubscription POST /2020-05-31/distributions/{DistributionId}/monitoring-subscription with required MonitoringSubscription (httpPayload), errors AccessDenied 403 / MonitoringSubscriptionAlreadyExists 409 / NoSuchDistribution 404 / UnsupportedOperation 400; GetMonitoringSubscription GET on the same URI, errors including NoSuchMonitoringSubscription 404; MonitoringSubscription.RealtimeMetricsSubscriptionConfig.RealtimeMetricsSubscriptionStatus is the Enabled|Disabled enum. The metric catalogue and the data plane are recorded behavior only: once a distribution is Deployed, a viewer GET to its published DomainName for a key the origin holds is answered 200 with the origin bytes (first request X-Cache: Miss from cloudfront, repeats Hit from cloudfront under a caching-enabled policy) and a GET for a key the origin does not hold is answered with the origin's refusal status (403 through an OAC granted s3:GetObject but not s3:ListBucket). Those served outcomes are metered in namespace AWS/CloudFront under the dimension pair DistributionId=<distribution id> + Region='Global'. The DEFAULT series — Requests (Sum) and 4xxErrorRate (Average) — are published for EVERY distribution; the ADDITIONAL series CacheHitRate (Average), OriginLatency (p90 extended statistic) and 403ErrorRate (Average) are published ONLY while that distribution's RealtimeMetricsSubscriptionStatus is Enabled, and are withheld entirely — no GetMetricStatistics datapoints and no ListMetrics entry — on a distribution with no monitoring subscription. The requirement is that gating relation plus the agreement of the default series with what the edge served (the check derives every expected value from the status codes and X-Cache headers it recorded, within its own slack of +/-1 request and 0.1 percentage points). Distribution ids, hosts, the particular minute and one run's counts are not requirements.

Emulator: the check produces no assertion result at all. The replay detail is `Error: Check timed out after 600.0s`, verdict mismatch. Its awaited effects are, in order: GetDistribution polled until Distribution.Status == 'Deployed' (1200 s) for both the subscribed distribution and the unsubscribed control; a bounded GET poll for a 200 from each distribution's published DomainName (300 s, 15 s per request); and a GetMetricStatistics poll in AWS/CloudFront for a first CacheHitRate datapoint on the SUBSCRIBED side (900 s at 30 s), followed by a differential barrier requiring, for one round minute, the subscribed side's CacheHitRate/403ErrorRate/OriginLatency datapoints AND the control's Requests/4xxErrorRate datapoints together with ListMetrics entries for both. The 600 s run budget expires inside those waits; no AWS/CloudFront datapoint is ever observed for either side, and the check never even reaches its own 900 s give-up point. The control-plane half that does answer is correct and is not the defect being reported: GetMonitoringSubscription on the subscribed distribution reads back 'Enabled', and on the control it is refused with code NoSuchMonitoringSubscription and the message 'The monitoring subscription does not exist.' (fixed text, no variable part). This is not a runner fault: every HTTP call carries a 15 s timeout and the check classifies a never-serving edge with its own DATAPLANE_UNIMPLEMENTED marker precisely so it reads as a fidelity failure rather than an environment error.

Why:      a client cannot tell whether the monitoring-subscription opt-in does anything. Dashboards and alarms built on CacheHitRate / OriginLatency / 403ErrorRate after enabling a subscription see permanently empty series and alarms over them sit in INSUFFICIENT_DATA instead of tracking the edge, so cache-tuning, paging and autoscaling keyed on them never fire. Symmetrically, nothing proves the additional metrics are WITHHELD without a subscription, so a client cannot rely on the opt-in as a cost/visibility boundary. More basically, the distribution is unusable: content published behind it cannot be fetched at the address CloudFront told the client to use.

Fix:      the CloudFront edge data plane — the listener/routing component that must actually serve viewer requests at the very address GetDistribution publishes as DomainName for a Deployed distribution, rather than only announcing it — plus the per-request outcome recording and the AWS/CloudFront metric-publication path that CreateMonitoringSubscription gates: it must publish Requests and 4xxErrorRate under DistributionId + Region='Global' for every distribution, and CacheHitRate, OriginLatency and 403ErrorRate (datapoints and ListMetrics entries alike) only while that distribution's RealtimeMetricsSubscriptionStatus is Enabled. Not the check, and not the GetMonitoringSubscription handler, which already answers correctly.

Traces:   cloudfront__cloudwatch.obs-cloudfront-1-additional-metrics-withheld-without-monitoring-subscription.
