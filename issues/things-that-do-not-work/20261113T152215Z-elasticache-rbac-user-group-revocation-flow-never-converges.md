Recorded behavior for the sequencing and the data plane: a replication group created with TransitEncryptionEnabled settles Status 'available', and the FIRST client read issued after a transition is due reports the settled value; ModifyReplicationGroup(UserGroupIdsToAdd=[g], ApplyImmediately=true) settles with g in ReplicationGroup$UserGroupIds and Status back to 'available'; ModifyUserGroup(UserIdsToRemove=[u]) moves the group to 'modifying' and back to 'active' WITHOUT u in UserIds; a TLS RESP session to NodeGroups[0].PrimaryEndpoint.{Address,Port} from inside the group's VPC then REFUSES that user's previously accepted AUTH with an error reply whose fixed wire text is `-WRONGPASS` (or `-NOAUTH` where no AUTH has succeeded on the connection), and ModifyUserGroup(UserIdsToAdd=[u]) settles 'active' with u restored and makes the IDENTICAL AUTH succeed again with `+OK`. The reply prefixes and the status words are the fixed text; the user id, group id, password, endpoint host and port are per-run values taken from the request and the emulator's state.

Emulator: the check is killed at the 60 s per-check budget with NO assertion evaluated — not the positive control (AUTH accepted while u is in the attached group), not the refusal after removal, not the restore after re-adding. The flow chains six sequential waits (DescribeReplicationGroups to 'available' at interval 20 s; DescribeReplicationGroups to available AND the group attached at 20 s; then DescribeUserGroups-active / DescribeReplicationGroups-available pairs at 15 s after each ModifyUserGroup) plus three in-VPC RESP sessions. Mechanism in this checkout: services/service-com-amazonaws-elasticache-amazonelasticachev9/src/state.rs:410 `pub const CLUSTER_TRANSITION: Duration = Duration::from_millis(500);` and :420 `pub const SNAPSHOT_TRANSITION: Duration = Duration::from_millis(500);`, documented at :400-420 as "deliberately NOT zero, so a caller acting straight after the call still observes the transient status and the state errors that go with it. The due instant is stored on the record and materialised by whichever later call reads it — nothing sleeps." A client's first poll after each create/modify therefore observes the transient status and sleeps its whole 15-20 s interval even though the emulator settled in 500 ms; four such misses already exceed the budget. Hypothesis: the three RESP sessions add the remainder, because the advertised PrimaryEndpoint accepts no TCP from inside the VPC (the probe retries a failed connect for a 60 s window before reporting), and the sibling data-plane checks of this same replay report the emulator's own connect refusal; the two contributions cannot be separated from this output, because no assertion in the check fired.

Why: RBAC user groups are the only mechanism by which an ElastiCache client scopes access to a replication group. Against this emulator a client cannot confirm any part of a revocation: not that removing a user from the attached group stops that user authenticating, not that re-adding it restores access, and not that the group and the replication group settle at all. A run killed by the clock is indistinguishable from a group that never settles and from an engine that authenticates everyone, so an access-revocation procedure validated against the emulator proves nothing, and SDK waiters sized for AWS intervals time out.

Fix: two components in the elasticache crate — (a) the status-settling path of its state (the settle/transition helpers around CLUSTER_TRANSITION, read by the DescribeReplicationGroups and DescribeUserGroups projections): a record whose transition instant has already passed must read back SETTLED to the FIRST call that observes it, while the create/modify RESPONSE keeps reporting the transient status other checks assert; and (b) the replication-group data plane, so the emulator serves RESP on the Address:Port its NodeGroups[].PrimaryEndpoint projection publishes and enforces the attached user group's membership there (a removed user's AUTH refused `-WRONGPASS`/`-NOAUTH`, a restored user's identical AUTH accepted `+OK`).
